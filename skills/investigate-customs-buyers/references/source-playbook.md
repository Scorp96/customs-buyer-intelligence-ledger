# Exhaustive Public Source Playbook

This playbook defines seed routes, not a fixed search budget. A result count, page count, elapsed time, high grade, or first positive item never proves completion.

## Source order and receipts

Prefer government/registry/court/regulator, official company pages/filings/accounts, attributable professional and partner sources, reputable industry/trade sources, then directories as leads. Capture the real Query or navigation action, start/end/check timestamps, tool/operator and execution ID, result count, replayable raw locator, SHA-256, generated Pivots and same-Owner Evidence IDs.

Run every applicable configured Source Family. `NOT_APPLICABLE` requires a country-specific reason. Login walls, 401/403/429, paywalls, captcha, anti-bot, dynamic-page failure and permission limits are `BLOCKED`.

Use `plan_public_source_calls` to obtain the current missing Source Families, network work and open-Pivot queries. The returned plan is not a search result and never proves coverage. Execute each applicable item with an actually visible host web/search/browser/public-registry/maps tool, then append only the real observed result, locator/snapshot and hash.

For public positive facts use `reference_type=PUBLIC_URL` and preserve the exact `http(s)` page. For user-supplied customs rows, local evidence files, provider receipts, calculations and legacy CRM history use the corresponding non-URL reference type and matching locator; never invent a URL to make a report look complete.

When Evidence is intended to support A/A+ commercial readiness, add only the compatible `commercial_gate_tags`. A tag is not a self-certification: Runtime checks its Module, Claim Type, grade, freshness, Owner and reference form. Official contact, decision-chain, contact-source and development-route gates also require a current claim-bound Information record; a homepage, search attempt or unbound person name is insufficient.

## Contact Source Profile

Cover the official home, Contact, About, footer and mobile layout; PDFs/catalogues/images/news/jobs; registry/tax/court/regulatory pages; Google and local maps; LinkedIn company and people; Facebook, Instagram, X and YouTube; explicit public WhatsApp and country-applicable Zalo; local directories, associations, exhibitions and chambers; reverse phone/email, domain and public MX; address/tax ID/alias/director reverse searches; supplier/Peer/partner referrals; and applicable public marketplace/store pages.

The profile may be expanded but never reduced. Finding an email, phone, person, form, social account or website creates Positive Evidence and usually new Pivots; it does not end contact coverage.

## Twelve modules

Execute and receipt:

1. Account history, Sent/Reply/Quote/Bounce/Refusal/Opt-out and enterprise locks.
2. Customs field scope, contamination, identifiers, logistics fields and arithmetic.
3. Legal and commercial entity resolution.
4. Importer of Record, Ultimate Buyer and actual material user.
5. Product identity, HS family, structure, use and falsification boundaries.
6. Business, product lines, scale, locations and operating status.
7. Dated trade continuity, supplier chain and competing suppliers.
8. Buying Group and decision roles.
9. Full Contact Source Profile.
10. Six-branch recursive network expansion.
11. Source coverage and conflict resolution.
12. Sales fit, CRM, history-safe next action and outreach readiness.

## Six network branches

For each Anchor execute regional peers, industry peers, same-scale companies, same-supplier buyers, same-product/HS/application buyers and competing suppliers/alternatives. Every discovered Peer needs independent Entity, Product, Trade/Business, Relationship, Company Profile, Contact Coverage and canonical dedup receipts. Only then decide promotion. Every promoted Peer becomes a new Anchor and repeats all six branches.

There is no fixed depth or total-Anchor number that can reject a qualified promotion or prove completion. When the current host lacks resources to continue, leave every unprocessed promoted Anchor in the queue and return `PAUSED_RESOURCE_LIMIT`.

## Pivot saturation

Record each high-yield alias, person, address, telephone, tax ID, domain, supplier, partner and Peer. A Pivot is consumed only by a later, different Attempt whose Query/navigation contains its value. The generating Attempt cannot consume it. Closure requires no open high-yield Pivots, no missing Peer receipts, an empty Anchor queue, completed cycle dedup, and an empty visual-check queue.

## Pause and resume

Keep per-site retries bounded and respect access controls. When a critical source cannot be legally accessed, return `INCOMPLETE_BLOCKED`. When the current task's available resources end, return `PAUSED_RESOURCE_LIMIT` with a resume queue. Neither state may be described as Complete or Negative entitled.

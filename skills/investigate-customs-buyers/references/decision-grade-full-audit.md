# Decision-Grade research contract for CBI FULL_AUDIT

This is an internal reference for the single `investigate-customs-buyers` Skill, adapted from the separately owned Decision-Grade Research research discipline. It **does not** create a second Plugin or research mode.

## 1. Identity first: mandatory ordered preflight
Bind (1) the legal/business entity and actual industry, (2) Google Maps/Business entity/address/website, (3) primary official website and all relevant contact/management pages, (4) official company Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn/X/local networks and employees/company-tagged people, and (5) EACH materially relevant named decision maker's own LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram/local profiles, role tenure and publicly attributable route. For every target stage, include exact URL/source, confidence, date and terminal or blocked status. A company page is never a personal account. A search snippet is a lead, not conclusive ownership evidence. Missing/blocked sources may not be reported as nonexistence.

## 2. Decision model and independent evidence
Define final commercial objective, material claims, alternatives and a falsifier before the deep search. Independently test at least company claims, public registry/address, trade/transaction, upstream/downstream, local language, competitor/substitute and decision-chain dimensions where they can change the decision. Group copied/aggregated/syndicated material into the same *evidence cluster* rather than counting page copies as independent confirmations.

Differentiate VERIFIED FACT, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, FORECAST, RECOMMENDATION and UNKNOWN/UNFINISHED. Normalize date type (publication, event, transaction, update), entity, unit, scope, geography and source owner. Preserve unresolved contradictions with links and timestamps.

## 3. Research saturation and EIV
After every meaningful acquisition batch, inspect the evidence ledger: which high-value material Claim is weakest, what alternative hypothesis could invalidate the decision, which Pivot is unconsumed, which network branch is unfinished, and what independent source could best change the answer? Rank by EIV (probability × decision impact × evidence-quality gain × commercial weight ÷ research cost). Do not declare Decision Saturation merely due to one strong answer, ten copied links, source-family checkmarks, one department contact, commercial grade or a count of peers. Every promoted Anchor's six branches remain claim/EIV-based.

## 4. Minimum ACTIVE research window
For a directly invoked substantive FULL_AUDIT, default to **28 minutes of useful active research** unless the user supplies another duration. Never idle, pad tool calls, artificially duplicate queries, or claim that a tool call ran in the background. If time cannot be measured, the runtime exits, tools block, the user stops or permission/budget limits halt useful progress, produce **INTERRUPTED**, **BLOCKED** or **PAUSED_RESOURCE_LIMIT**, not `research_complete`. Duration alone cannot prove saturation even if measured. Current CBI Runtime exposes this as host research policy; it does not mechanically attest duration.

## 5. Fail-closed contacts and operations
Do not invent a named person, a social profile, social handle, phone, email or WhatsApp ownership. A 403, login wall, empty search or timeout is SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE/BLOCKED; try independent search paths. Historical, supplier-owned, logistics-owned, masked, guessed or company-general contact is not a named buyer's private route. Do not auto-write CRM, consume paid provider credits, start monitoring, create executable send links or send outreach without explicit scope permission.

## 6. Report only what was achieved
A complete result requires valid CBI Closure plus sufficient independent evidence and credible active window, and no material open contradictory Claim/Pivot. Otherwise provide partial findings with exact sources and an honest **中断交接报告** including current target, canonical account/investigation ID if any, last verified sources, completed stages, incomplete people/social channels, live blockers, counterhypotheses, highest-EIV next research objective, and a precise resume plan. Never invent Evidence/Closure IDs or claim that ChatGPT is still working after the response ends.
